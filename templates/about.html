About The Project

AI-powered career guidance for the modern workforce.

Project Overview

The Adaptive AI Skill Gap Analyzer is an intelligent platform designed to help students and job seekers bridge the gap between their current skills and the requirements of their target career roles. The system uses a Decision Tree-based machine learning model to compare user skills with industry-required competencies, calculate match scores, identify missing skills, and generate personalized learning roadmaps with Explainable AI (XAI) insights.

Objectives

  • Analyze current skills against industry-required competencies
  • Calculate match score and skill gap percentage
  • Identify missing and critical skills for target roles
  • Generate personalized, AI-driven learning roadmaps
  • Provide Explainable AI (XAI) reasoning for recommendations
  • Assess knowledge through interactive quizzes with performance tracking
